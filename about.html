Our story

House of Perfection is a tranquil and professional advanced skin and body clinic. We have been operating for 22 years and currently have 2 qualified therapists with over 20 years experience.

We are committed to giving you personalized, professional treatments to cater for your individual needs. For your convenience, we offer Saturday and after hour treatments to suit your working hours.

We can create tailor-made packages to suit your needs and budget. Gift Vouchers are also available to spoil the special people in your life.
