Privacy Policy

How House of Perfection handles your personal information. Last updated: November 2026.

House of Perfection (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information we collect

When you book a treatment or contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or the booking form on this website, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment time and the treatment you are interested in. For skin and body treatments we may also record information about your skin and relevant health details so that we can treat you safely.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and manage your appointments.
  • To recommend and safely perform suitable treatments.
  • To answer your questions and send you information you have asked for, such as our price list or gift vouchers.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Sharing and storage

We only share your information with service providers that help us run our business (for example email and messaging services), and only as far as needed. We keep your information only for as long as it is needed for these purposes or as required by law, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us using it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
