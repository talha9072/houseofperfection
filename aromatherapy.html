What is an aromatherapy massage?

Aromatherapy massage combines the calming touch of a professional massage with the scent and properties of pure essential oils. The oils are blended into the massage medium and gently worked into the skin while you breathe in their aroma, so the treatment relaxes both body and mind.

At House of Perfection we use pure essential oils in our massage blends to relax, heal and de-stress. It is the ideal treatment if you carry tension in your back, neck and shoulders, or simply need an hour to let go, relax and recharge.

Choose your massage

  • Back massage (30 minutes): focused relief for tight back, neck and shoulder muscles.
  • Hot stones back massage (30 minutes): warm stones melt away deeper tension.
  • Back treatment (45 minutes): a scrub, masque and massage that leaves the skin on your back smooth and refreshed.
  • Feet and lower legs massage (20 minutes): a relaxing add-on during any facial.

Why book a massage?

Regular massage can help reduce stress and muscle tension, support circulation and improve sleep quality. Many of our clients pair a massage with a facial for a complete pamper session, and massages also make a thoughtful gift voucher.
